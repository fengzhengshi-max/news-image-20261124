## Lead — Nvidia brings back Huang (Mon 23 Nov reinstatement, Tue 24 Nov fallout)

  two weeks after he resigned after admitting to an affair with Alice

  health care Kimberly Powell. Trump said he "made the deal" with
  Nvidia's board to get Huang his job back; VP JD Vance worked

  independence for reinstating Huang before investigations concluded.

## Grid 5 — US consumer confidence jumps (Reuters, Tue 24 Nov)
- US consumer confidence surged in November, touching its highest
  level in nearly two years. The Conference Board index jumped to

  92.5. Optimism over labour market conditions and the economy
  followed this month's midterm elections.
- Source: Reuters, 24 Nov.
